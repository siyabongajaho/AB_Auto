Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located and what are your operating hours?

We are proudly based in Welkom. Please visit our contact section for our exact address and current operating hours, or give us a call to book an appointment.

What types of vehicles do you service?

Our qualified mechanics service all major makes and models, including passenger cars, SUVs, and light commercial vehicles (bakkies).

Do you provide a quote before starting any mechanical work?

Yes. We believe in complete transparency. We will always diagnose your vehicle and provide a detailed quote for your approval before any repairs begin.

How often should I bring my car in for a routine service?

We generally recommend a minor service every 10,000 to 15,000 kilometres or once a year, depending on your manufacturer's specific guidelines and your driving habits.
